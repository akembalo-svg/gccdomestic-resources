# What is Tadbeer?

**Tadbeer** (Arabic: تدبير, "arrangement") is a regulated network of approximately 240 MOHRE-licensed recruitment centres in the UAE that manage the legal recruitment, sponsorship, and welfare of domestic workers.

**Full definitional page:** https://www.gccdomestic.com/en/what-is-tadbeer/

## Quick facts

- 🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates
- Regulator: MOHRE (Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation)
- Established: January 2018
- Legal basis: UAE Federal Law No. 10 of 2017
- ~240 centres across 7 emirates
- Recruitment fee: AED 12,000-18,000 (one-time)
- 6-month replacement warranty (mandatory)

## Verify any centre

1. mohre.gov.ae → Establishment Search
2. Enter centre name or facility number
3. Confirm "Active" status
4. Call MOHRE 600-590-000

## Vs Musaned (Saudi Arabia)

Tadbeer is a network of physical centres. [Musaned](https://www.gccdomestic.com/en/what-is-musaned/) is a centralised electronic platform. Different structures, same purpose.
