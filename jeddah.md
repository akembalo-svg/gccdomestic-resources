# 🌊 Hiring a Domestic Worker in Jeddah

Jeddah, Saudi Arabia's commercial capital, has 150+ Musaned-licensed agencies and a slightly more cosmopolitan recruitment market than Riyadh due to its long history as a port city and Hajj gateway.

## Jeddah specifics

- 🌐 **More diverse expat density** than Riyadh — agencies cater to mix of Saudi and Asian/African expats
- 🕌 **Hajj-season demand spike** — recruitment activity peaks 2-3 months before/after Hajj
- 💰 **Slightly higher pricing** than Riyadh due to cost of living
- 🍽️ **Strong demand for cooks** — Jeddahi cuisine specialization is a value-add

## Cost in Jeddah (2026)

| Service | One-time fee | Monthly salary |
|---|---|---|
| Filipino housemaid | SAR 8,500–16,000 | SAR 1,300–1,900 |
| Indonesian housemaid | SAR 7,500–13,000 | SAR 1,200–1,700 |
| Bangladeshi housemaid | SAR 6,500–12,000 | SAR 850–1,300 |

## Top Jeddah neighborhoods

- **Al Hamra / Corniche:** premium agencies for high-end residences
- **Al Salama / Al Faisaliyah:** family-friendly central options
- **Al Naseem:** affordable budget tier
