# 🕌 Hiring a Domestic Worker in Sharjah

Sharjah, the UAE's third-largest emirate, has 15+ Tadbeer centers serving a more conservative cultural demographic than Dubai or Abu Dhabi. Family-focused, Muslim-majority demand drives strong demand for Indonesian, Bangladeshi, and Ethiopian Muslim workers.

## Sharjah specifics

- ☪️ **Conservative cultural fit** — preference for hijab-wearing Muslim workers
- 💰 **Most affordable in the UAE** — typically 15-20% less than Dubai
- 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 **Large-family households** — Sharjah families are often larger, demand more workers per household
- 🌃 **Stricter alcohol laws** — affects worker preferences in some cases

## Cost in Sharjah (2026)

| Service | One-time fee | Monthly salary |
|---|---|---|
| Indonesian housemaid | AED 7,000–12,000 | AED 1,300–2,000 |

| Ethiopian housemaid (Muslim) | AED 5,500–10,000 | AED 1,000–1,500 |
| Filipino nanny | AED 8,000–14,000 | AED 1,500–2,500 |

## Top areas

- **Al Majaz / Al Khan:** largest Tadbeer cluster
- **Al Nahda:** family-friendly with multiple options
- **Sharjah Industrial Area:** budget-friendly centers
