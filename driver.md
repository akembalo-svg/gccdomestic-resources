# 🚗 Hire a Driver in the GCC — 2026 Guide

A family or personal driver is a common need across the GCC, especially for women in conservative households, school runs, and elderly care. India dominates the driver recruitment corridor.

## Driver requirements

- 🪪 **Valid driving license** from source country
- 🚦 **Gulf-country license conversion** (usually within 30 days of arrival)
- 🧐 **Clean driving record** (verifiable)
- 🚙 **Familiarity with manual + automatic** (manual preferred for some employers)
- 🗺️ **Local geography knowledge** (Dubai, Riyadh, Doha layouts memorized within first week)

## What a driver actually does

- 🏫 School run (morning + afternoon)
- 🛍️ Shopping + errands
- 👵 Hospital + clinic visits for elderly
- 💼 Office commute for the head of household
- ✈️ Airport pickups + drop-offs
- 🧼 Often expected to maintain vehicle cleanliness

## Important notes

- Most GCC countries require driver to convert their license within 30 days
- Driver insurance is separate — clarify whether employer or worker pays
- Many employers provide accommodation + transport during off-hours
