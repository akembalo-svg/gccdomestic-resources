# 🌧️ Hiring a Domestic Worker in Salalah (Dhofar)

Salalah, capital of the Dhofar region in southern Oman, has ~40+ MoL-licensed offices. The recruitment market here is shaped by the **Khareef monsoon tourism season** (June-September) which drives demand for hospitality + private household staff.

## Salalah specifics

- 🌧️ **Khareef season demand peak** — June through September sees increased recruitment
- 🏝️ **Lower year-round demand** than Muscat but very stable
- 💰 **Most affordable in Oman** — Dhofar prices typically 10-15% below Muscat
- 🌍 **East African corridors strong** — Ethiopian, Kenyan workers common

## Cost in Salalah (2026)

| Service | One-time fee | Monthly salary |
|---|---|---|
| Ethiopian housemaid | OMR 180–340 | OMR 60–85 |
| Indian housemaid | OMR 230–420 | OMR 70–105 |
| Filipino housemaid | OMR 320–550 | OMR 85–125 |
