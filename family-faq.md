# ❓ Family FAQ — Hiring Domestic Workers in the GCC

## How much does it really cost?

Total Year-1 cost (one-time + 12 months salary):

- **UAE (Tadbeer Filipino housemaid):** AED 22,400 – 49,200
- **Saudi (Musaned Filipino housemaid):** SAR 19,200 – 31,500
- **Kuwait (Filipino housemaid):** KWD 1,560 – 2,760
- **Qatar (Filipino housemaid):** QAR 21,500 – 36,000
- **Bahrain (Filipino housemaid):** BHD 1,710 – 2,820
- **Oman (Filipino housemaid):** OMR 1,170 – 1,860

Full breakdown by nationality + role: [Salary benchmarks](/salaries/)

## How long does the process take?

- **UAE Tadbeer:** 4-8 weeks
- **Saudi Musaned:** 6-12 weeks
- **Kuwait PAM:** 4-6 weeks
- **Qatar / Oman / Bahrain:** 6-10 weeks

Anyone promising "tomorrow" is offering an illegal back-channel — don't pay.

## Can I hire directly without an agency?

In most GCC countries: **no longer.** UAE Federal Decree-Law 9/2022 channels nearly all new hiring through Tadbeer. Saudi Musaned is mandatory. The few legal direct-sponsorship paths (GCC nationals, certain professional categories) are exceptions.

Read: [Tadbeer vs Direct Sponsorship in UAE](https://www.gccdomestic.com/en/blog/tadbeer-vs-direct-sponsorship-uae/)

## What if I don't like the worker?

Every legitimate agency offers a **replacement window** — typically 3-6 months. Within that window:
- Either party can end the contract
- The agency provides a free replacement
- You can request a different nationality

This is the single most important contract term to verify before paying.

## What rights does the worker have?

Different per country but the regional minimums (post-2017 reforms):
- Weekly day off
- Paid annual leave
- Minimum wage (set by the source country's labour attaché)
- Right to keep their passport (illegal for the employer to confiscate)
- Access to government complaint channels

## What if my hire absconds (runs away)?

Report to the relevant authority within 48 hours:
- UAE: MOHRE absconding report
- Saudi: Absher / Musaned absconding portal
- Kuwait: PAM absconding form
- Other: Ministry of Labour

The agency-paid bond covers part of your re-recruitment cost if the absconding is verified.

## Can I bring a worker from a country other than the agency's roster?

In theory yes, in practice rare. Agencies specialize by nationality because they have established corridors with source-country government channels (POEA in Philippines, BNP2TKI in Indonesia, etc.). Filter by nationality when picking your agency.
