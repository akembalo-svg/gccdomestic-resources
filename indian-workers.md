# 🇮🇳 Indian Domestic Workers in the Gulf

India is one of the largest source nations for Gulf domestic workers, particularly drivers, cooks, and household staff. Indian workers come from many states — Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, and Karnataka dominate the recruitment corridors.

## Why Gulf families choose Indian workers

- 🗣️ **English-speaking** (especially Kerala, Tamil Nadu) — communicates well with expat families
- 🍳 **Strong cooking skills** — comfortable with Indian, continental, and Arabic cuisine
- 🚗 **Driver corridor** — India is the dominant source for personal drivers in the Gulf
- 💰 **Mid-tier salary range** — between Filipino and Ethiopian price points

## Indian hiring process

1. **POE (Protector of Emigrants)** clearance via eMigrate portal — mandatory for emigration clearance required (ECR) categories
2. **Recruitment Agent (RA)** must be MEA-registered (Ministry of External Affairs)
3. **Pre-departure orientation** at PMSBY-approved centers
4. **Visa stamping** through Gulf country consulate in India
5. **Embassy registration** in destination country

Typical timeline: **4-8 weeks** across all GCC countries.
