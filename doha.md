# 🌟 Hiring a Domestic Worker in Doha

Doha hosts most of Qatar's 74+ MoL-licensed domestic worker agencies. Qatar's Law 15/2017 set the regional benchmark for worker protections, making Doha one of the more transparent recruitment markets in the Gulf.

## Doha specifics

- 🌍 **Diplomatic + expat heavy** — agencies serve UN, diplomatic, and Fortune-500 expat communities
- ⚽ **Post-World Cup labour reforms** — strong oversight following 2022 World Cup attention
- 💼 **High-end nanny demand** — certified childcare workers in high demand
- 🍳 **Multi-cuisine expectation** — international families need workers comfortable with diverse food
