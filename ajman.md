# 🌆 Hiring a Domestic Worker in Ajman

Ajman, the smallest of the seven UAE emirates, has a tight cluster of Tadbeer centers — typically the most affordable in the country. Many families in Sharjah and even Dubai cross to Ajman for cost-conscious recruitment.

## Ajman specifics

- 💰 **Most affordable Tadbeer fees in UAE** — typically 10-20% cheaper than Dubai
- 🚗 **Easy access from Sharjah, Umm Al Quwain, Ras Al Khaimah**
- 👨‍👩‍👧 **Strong family demand**
- ☪️ **Indonesian and Bangladeshi corridors well-developed**

## Cost in Ajman (2026)

| Service | One-time fee | Monthly salary |
|---|---|---|
| Filipino housemaid | AED 8,000–14,000 | AED 1,500–2,400 |
| Indonesian housemaid | AED 7,500–12,000 | AED 1,300–2,000 |
| Ethiopian housemaid | AED 5,500–10,000 | AED 1,000–1,500 |
