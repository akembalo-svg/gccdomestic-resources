# 🕋 Hiring a Domestic Worker in Makkah (Mecca)

Makkah hosts ~50+ Musaned-licensed agencies serving the city's residents, with seasonal demand spikes tied to Hajj and Ramadan-Umrah peaks. Most agencies specialize in workers comfortable with Muslim households and Arabic-speaking families.

## Makkah specifics

- ☪️ **Strong preference for Muslim workers** (Indonesian, Bangladeshi, Ethiopian Muslim)
- 🕌 **Hajj-season demand peak** — September-November sees ~3x normal recruitment activity
- 🏨 **Hotel/serviced apartment demand** — many workers placed with hospitality businesses near Haram
- 💰 **Slightly lower salaries** than Riyadh/Jeddah but comparable one-time fees

## Cost in Makkah (2026)

| Service | One-time fee | Monthly salary |
|---|---|---|
| Indonesian housemaid | SAR 7,000–12,000 | SAR 1,100–1,600 |
| Bangladeshi housemaid | SAR 6,000–11,000 | SAR 800–1,200 |
| Ethiopian (Muslim) housemaid | SAR 5,000–10,000 | SAR 900–1,400 |
