# 🇧🇩 Bangladeshi Domestic Workers in the Gulf

Bangladesh is a major source of domestic workers for the Gulf, especially Saudi Arabia where Bangladeshi housemaids form one of the largest national groups. Most workers come from rural Bangladesh and are Muslim-majority.

## Why Gulf families choose Bangladeshi workers

- ☪️ **Muslim-majority** — fits Saudi/Kuwait/UAE preferences for religious continuity
- 💰 **Lower salary tier** — most competitive pricing among nationalities
- 🤝 **Strong family-care orientation**
- ⏰ **Faster process** — bilateral agreements have streamlined Bangladesh-Saudi corridor

## Bangladeshi hiring process

1. **BMET (Bureau of Manpower, Employment & Training)** clearance — mandatory smart card
2. **RA (Recruitment Agency)** in Bangladesh must be BAIRA-registered
3. **3-day pre-departure training** at BMET-approved centers
4. **Departure via Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport** (Dhaka)
5. **Embassy registration** in destination country

Timeline: **4-7 weeks** for Saudi, 6-10 weeks for other GCC.

## Post-2015 Saudi reopening context

After Indonesia and Philippines tightened or restricted Saudi recruitment in 2015-2017, Bangladesh became the dominant new source for Saudi domestic workers. The 2015 Bangladesh-Saudi bilateral memorandum:
- Set minimum wage at SAR 800/month for housemaids
- Required Bangladesh embassy oversight for major contract disputes
- Mandatory accident insurance
- Worker-friendly transfer-of-sponsorship clauses
