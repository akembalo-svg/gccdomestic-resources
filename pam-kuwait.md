# 🇰🇼 Kuwait PAM — Public Authority for Manpower

PAM (Public Authority for Manpower) is Kuwait's regulator for all foreign labour, including domestic workers. **Kuwait's Domestic Workers Law 68/2015** is one of the strongest in the Gulf, with mandatory weekly day off, paid annual leave, and end-of-service indemnity.

## What PAM regulates

- Recruitment office licensing
- Worker visa issuance
- Contract templates
- Salary disputes
- End-of-service settlements
- Worker transfer between sponsors

## Kuwait Domestic Workers Law 68/2015 — key protections

- ✅ Weekly day off (mandatory)
- ✅ 30 days paid annual leave
- ✅ 12-hour maximum work day with rest periods
- ✅ End-of-service indemnity (1 month per year of service)
- ✅ Workers can file court complaints directly
- ✅ Employers cannot confiscate passports

## Process via PAM

1. Family contacts a PAM-licensed office
2. Office secures worker visa + medical
3. Standard contract signed (PAM template)
4. Worker arrives
5. Civil ID issued
6. Monthly salary paid (preferably via bank transfer for paper trail)
