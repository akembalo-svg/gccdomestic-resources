# 🛢️ Hiring a Domestic Worker in Dammam & Al Khobar

Saudi Arabia's Eastern Province — Dammam, Al Khobar, Dhahran — concentrates the oil industry and is home to high expat density (Aramco, Sabic, Saudi Electricity). The metro area has ~70+ Musaned-licensed agencies serving both Saudi and expat families.

## Eastern Province specifics

- 🌍 **High expat density** — Aramco, multinational oil firms
- 💼 **Professional household demand** — many dual-income families
- 🍽️ **Multi-cuisine expectation** — workers comfortable with Asian, Arabic, Western food
- 💰 **Higher salaries** than other Saudi cities due to expat market
