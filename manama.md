# 🏝️ Hiring a Domestic Worker in Manama

Manama, Bahrain's capital, hosts most of the country's 30+ LMRA-licensed offices. Bahrain pioneered the **Flexi Permit (2017)** allowing workers to work without a single sponsor, and the **2022 transfer reforms** strengthened mobility rights.

## Manama specifics

- 🏛️ **LMRA centralized oversight** — strong digital infrastructure for visa + permit
- 🚀 **2017 Flexi Permit** — regional first, allows workers to multi-employer
- 🌐 **Smaller market but better-regulated** than other GCC capitals
- 💰 **Affordable mid-tier pricing**
