# ⚖️ Domestic Worker Rights — GCC Overview

The 2010s-2020s saw significant legal reforms for domestic workers across the Gulf. This is a country-by-country summary of where each one stands in 2026.

## Universal regional benchmarks (post-2017)

Most GCC countries now require:
- ✅ Written contract
- ✅ Minimum wage (varies by source country)
- ✅ Weekly day off
- ✅ Paid annual leave
- ✅ Maximum daily working hours (typically 8-12)
- ✅ Worker keeps their own passport
- ✅ Access to formal complaint channels

## 🇦🇪 UAE — Federal Decree-Law 9/2022

- One-day-per-week off (minimum)
- 30 days annual paid leave
- Sick leave up to 30 days/year
- End-of-service gratuity
- Tadbeer-mediated dispute resolution
- Anti-confiscation: employer cannot keep worker's passport

File a complaint: MOHRE app or 800-60

## 🇸🇦 Saudi Arabia — Domestic Workers Regulation (2013, updated 2024)

- Weekly day off
- 1 month paid leave after 2 years of service
- Minimum wage set by source-country embassy
- Banned: passport confiscation
- Musaned platform tracks contract status
- Workers can transfer between sponsors with conditions

File a complaint: Musaned app or 19911

## 🇰🇼 Kuwait — Law 68/2015

- One of the region's strongest domestic worker laws
- Mandatory weekly day off
- 30 days paid annual leave
- 12-hour max work day with rest periods
- End-of-service indemnity
- Workers can file court complaints

File a complaint: PAM 134

## 🇶🇦 Qatar — Law 15/2017

- Sets the regional benchmark for protections
- Maximum 10 hours work/day
- 3 weeks paid annual leave
- Workers can change employers after expiry without sponsor permission (2020 reform)
- End-of-service payment
- Banned: passport confiscation

File a complaint: ADLSA / 16008

## 🇧🇭 Bahrain — Flexi Permit + 2022 reforms

- Flexi Permit allows workers to work without a sponsor (regional first, 2017)
- 2022 reforms strengthened transfer rights
- LMRA monitors recruitment offices
- Weekly day off mandatory

File a complaint: LMRA 17506055

## 🇴🇲 Oman — Royal Decree 35/2003 + supplementary regulations

- Standard contract template required
- Weekly day off
- Annual leave
- End-of-service indemnity

File a complaint: Ministry of Labour 80077000

## What to do if a worker's rights are being violated

1. **Document everything in writing** (WhatsApp messages, emails)
2. **Contact the relevant country authority** (numbers above)
3. **Contact the worker's source-country embassy** (Philippines POEA, Indonesia BNP2TKI, Ethiopia MoL, etc.)
4. **Email us:** **info@gccdomestic.com** — we can help connect to legal aid resources

This page is a reference, not legal advice. For specific situations, consult an attorney licensed in the relevant country.
