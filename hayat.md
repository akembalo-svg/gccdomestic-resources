# 🌟 Hayat — Your Family's AI Domestic Helper

**Caring. Intelligent. Culturally aware.**

Hayat (حياة) is the first AI domestic helper built specifically for Gulf families. She lives on WhatsApp and speaks Gulf-Arabic the way an actual auntie speaks it — not the textbook fusha Arabic that Alexa/Siri force on the region.

## What makes Hayat different

| Other "Arabic AI" | Hayat |
|---|---|
| Speaks fusha (textbook Arabic) | Speaks Gulf-Arabic (Khaliji dialect) |
| Doesn't know prayer times | Knows Maghrib, Asr, Fajr, Isha, Dhuhr per city |
| Treats Ramadan as a banner | Ramadan is a calendar mode (system behavior changes) |
| Suggests pork recipes | Halal-by-default |
| Built by US/EU teams | Built in Dubai, for Gulf families |

## What Hayat does

- 🏠 **Household management** — schedules, shopping lists, driver routes, agency comms
- 👶 **Childcare context** (as **Hayat Umm**) — tracks feeds, sleep, vaccinations, milestones
- 🍳 **Iftar planning** — full menu + prep order + shopping list in 2 messages
- 🙏 **Prayer-aware** — never schedules conflicting meetings during prayer times
- 🌙 **Ramadan-aware** — adjusts meal planning, work schedules, and tone during the holy month
- 💬 **8-language fluency** — Arabic, English, Tagalog, Hindi, Amharic, Swahili, Indonesian, Urdu

## How families use Hayat

Most common requests we see:

1. *"What do I cook for ten people for iftar tomorrow using the lamb in the freezer and no oven?"* → menu + prep + shopping list in 2 messages
2. *"My maid's contract ends in 60 days. What do I need to do?"* → step-by-step renewal process
3. *"I need a Filipino nanny in Dubai for a 6-month-old, budget AED 2,500/month"* → 3-5 matched Tadbeer agencies
4. *"What time does my baby usually wake up?"* (Hayat Umm) → schedule + sleep regression alerts
5. *"Today's Maghrib in Riyadh?"* → instant + scheduled meal-reminder accordingly

## Tech behind Hayat

- **Channel:** WhatsApp Business API (no app required)
- **Languages:** Gulf-Arabic fine-tuned on regional X/Twitter + voice transcripts
- **Privacy:** zero data sharing with third parties
- **Free for families forever** (agencies pay for verified listings, not families)
