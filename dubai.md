# 🏙️ Hiring a Domestic Worker in Dubai

Dubai has the largest concentration of Tadbeer centers in the UAE — over 60 of the 132+ MOHRE-licensed centers operate within the emirate. This is your complete 2026 guide.

## Why Dubai is different

- 🌍 **Highest expat density** in the GCC — most diverse demand for nationalities and languages
- 🚇 **Geographic spread** — agencies cluster in Bur Dubai, Karama, Deira, Al Quoz, Dubai Marina
- 💼 **Higher salaries** — Dubai pays the top of the UAE range due to cost of living
- 🤝 **Strong replacement guarantee culture** — Tadbeer competition keeps standards high

## Dubai-specific Tadbeer notes

- All new hires must go through Tadbeer (Federal Decree-Law 9/2022)
- Direct sponsorship now restricted to UAE nationals + specific professional categories
- Tadbeer centers in Dubai International Financial Centre (DIFC) often serve expat executives

## Top neighborhoods + their Tadbeer clusters

- **Bur Dubai / Karama:** highest concentration, most affordable
- **Al Quoz:** mid-tier, easier parking
- **Dubai Marina / JLT:** premium centers catering to high-end residences
- **Mirdif / International City:** family-friendly clusters
