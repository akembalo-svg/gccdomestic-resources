# 🍳 Hire a Cook in the GCC — 2026 Guide

A dedicated cook is a step above a housemaid who does basic cooking. Cooks specialize in regional cuisines and often have professional kitchen training.

## Cook specializations

- 🇮🇳 **Indian/South Indian** — most common, broad menu (biryani, dosa, curry, kebab)
- 🇵🇭 **Filipino** — multicultural (Filipino + Western + Asian)
- 🇱🇰 **Sri Lankan** — rice + curry specialist
- 🇪🇹 **Ethiopian** — for households appreciating East African flavors
- ☪️ **Halal-by-default** — most workers respect this even if not personally Muslim

## What a cook actually does

- 🍳 All meals — breakfast, lunch, dinner
- 🛒 Menu planning + shopping list
- 🧊 Kitchen organization + meal prep
- 🍰 Special-occasion cooking (Eid, Ramadan iftar, dinner parties)
- 👨‍🍳 Sometimes light cleaning of kitchen only (not full household)
