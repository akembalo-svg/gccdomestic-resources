# 🧹 Hire a Housemaid in the GCC — 2026 Guide

The most common domestic worker role across all 6 GCC countries. This is your end-to-end guide.

## Housemaid responsibilities (standard)

- 🧹 Cleaning (rooms, kitchen, bathrooms)
- 🧺 Laundry and ironing
- 🍳 Basic cooking (more for cooks)
- 🛒 Shopping with employer's permission/list
- 👶 Light childcare assistance (not certified nanny)
- 🪴 Plant care, light pet care

## Common questions

- **"Can the housemaid also cook?"** Yes for basic meals; for advanced cooking hire a [cook](/cook/) instead
- **"Can she watch my kids?"** Light supervision yes; for childcare hire a [nanny](/nanny/) (certified)
- **"What about her day off?"** Mandatory across all 6 GCC countries — varies from 1 day/week to flexible
