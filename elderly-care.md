# 👵 Hire an Elderly Care Worker in the GCC — 2026 Guide

Elderly care is a specialized role demanding patience, basic medical knowledge, and often Arabic language skills. Sri Lankan and Filipino workers dominate this corridor.

## What elderly care workers do

- 🩺 **Medication management** — schedules, reminders, dosage tracking
- 🚿 **Personal hygiene assistance** — bathing, dressing, mobility help
- 🍽️ **Meal preparation** specific to dietary restrictions (diabetic, low-sodium, etc.)
- 🧘 **Companionship** — reading, conversation, light activities
- 🚑 **Emergency response** — first aid, calling family, ambulance coordination
- 📋 **Coordination with medical staff** — doctor visits, lab tests, physiotherapy

## Required qualifications

- ✅ Basic First Aid + CPR certification (most important)
- ✅ Experience with elderly (minimum 1 year, verifiable)
- ✅ Patience verification (interview-based)
- ✅ Arabic basic communication (helpful for Gulf Arabic-speaking elderly)
- ⚕️ Light nursing training (preferable, not mandatory)

## Cultural fit matters more here

- Female elderly often prefer female workers (and vice versa)
- Religious + cultural compatibility is more important than for general housemaid roles
- Workers who speak some Arabic + the elderly person's first language are most valued
