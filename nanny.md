# 👶 Hire a Nanny in the GCC — 2026 Guide

Nannies differ from housemaids — they are **certified childcare specialists**, typically with TESDA (Philippines), childcare diplomas, or equivalent qualifications. Most Gulf nannies are Filipino, with growing demand for Indonesian and Kenyan nannies.

## Nanny qualifications to look for

- 🎓 TESDA Certificate (Philippines) — most common
- 👶 Early Childhood Development (ECD) qualification
- 🩺 First Aid certification
- 🇬🇧 English fluency
- 📚 References from prior childcare role (minimum 1 year)

## Nanny vs housemaid — what's the difference

| Aspect | Housemaid | Nanny |
|---|---|---|
| Primary role | Cleaning | Childcare |
| Certification | Not required | TESDA / ECD typically |
| Salary | Lower tier | Higher tier (+30-50%) |
| Hours | Flexible | Often follows kids' schedule |
| Training | General household | Child psychology + safety |

## How to hire a nanny

[7-step process →](/process/) — same as for any domestic worker, but with these extra checks:
1. **Verify TESDA / ECD certificate** — ask for original docs, not photos
2. **Reference call** — ask the previous family directly
3. **Video interview before contract** — assess interaction with your kids
4. **Trial period** — first 30 days as probation
