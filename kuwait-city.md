# 🌃 Hiring a Domestic Worker in Kuwait City

Kuwait City concentrates most of the country's 50+ PAM-licensed offices. Kuwait's Law 68/2015 is among the strongest domestic worker laws in the Gulf — mandatory weekly day off, end-of-service indemnity, and direct court access for workers.

## Kuwait City specifics

- 💪 **Strong worker protections** under Law 68/2015
- 👨‍👩‍👧 **Large Kuwaiti family demand** — multi-worker households common
- 💰 **Mid-tier salaries** in the GCC range
- 🍽️ **Strong Filipino, Indian, Sri Lankan corridors**
