# 🇦🇪 Tadbeer Centers — The UAE Hiring Channel

Since **Federal Decree-Law 9/2022**, nearly all new domestic worker hiring in the UAE must go through Tadbeer centers — MOHRE-licensed agencies that handle visa, contract, training, and after-care.

## What is Tadbeer?

Tadbeer (تدبير) means "management" in Arabic. The Tadbeer system was launched in 2018 and made mandatory for most categories in 2022. It centralizes:
- Visa processing
- Standard MOHRE contract
- Pre-arrival training in source country
- Post-arrival orientation
- Replacement guarantee (3-6 months)
- Dispute mediation

## How to verify a Tadbeer center

1. Ask for their **license number** (looks like "TDB-XXXX")
2. Check it against the [MOHRE Tadbeer portal](https://www.mohre.gov.ae)
3. Cross-reference with our directory: [132+ verified Tadbeer centers](https://www.gccdomestic.com/uae/agencies/)

If an "agency" in the UAE is offering domestic worker hiring WITHOUT a Tadbeer license, **it's almost certainly illegal**. Report to MOHRE: 800-60.

## What Tadbeer covers vs doesn't

✅ Includes:
- Worker recruitment from source country
- 2-year visa
- Medical fitness test
- Emirates ID
- Standard MOHRE contract
- 3-6 month replacement guarantee
- Pre-arrival training (basic Arabic, household orientation)
- Post-arrival home visit

❌ Doesn't include:
- Monthly salary (paid separately)
- Worker's annual leave airfare (employer's responsibility)
- Medical insurance beyond visa requirements
- End-of-service gratuity
