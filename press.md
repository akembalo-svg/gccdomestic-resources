## Talking points (frequent journalist questions)

### "What problem does GCC Domestic solve?"

The Gulf recruitment industry for domestic workers has been opaque for decades — agency fees vary widely, license verification is hard for families, and abuse cases have been historically under-tracked. GCC Domestic indexes every agency with its government license, so every claim is checkable.

### "Why AI in this space?"

Two reasons:
1. Most "Arabic AI" was built by teams who don't live in the Gulf. They speak fusha (textbook Arabic), not Gulf-Arabic. They don't know when Maghrib is. They suggest pork recipes.
2. Gulf families already live in WhatsApp — adding another app is friction. AI helpers on WhatsApp meet families where they already are.

### "How is Hayat different from Alexa/Siri/ChatGPT?"

Hayat is built specifically for Gulf households:
- Speaks Gulf-Arabic the way an actual auntie speaks it
- Knows prayer times (Maghrib, Asr, Fajr, Isha, Dhuhr) per city
- Treats Ramadan as a calendar mode (system behavior changes), not a banner
- Halal-by-default — never suggests pork or alcohol recipes
- Tracks household-specific context: kids' schedules, driver routes, agency communications

### "What's the founder's background?"

Ibrahim Kedir is the founder & CEO, based in Dubai. He built GCC Domestic in 2024 after seeing the gap between Western-built AI tools and what Gulf families actually need.

### "Who are your competitors?"

The recruitment side competes with traditional agency directories like Tadbeer-only listings or Justlife (cleaning-hourly). The AI side has no direct competitor in Gulf-Arabic conversational AI. Hayat is the first of its kind.

### "What are your numbers?"

We don't publish specific revenue or user numbers, but we can confirm:
- Indexing 1,700+ agencies
- Operating across all 6 GCC countries
- Quietly profitable since month 4
- 30,000+ WhatsApp conversations to date

### "Have there been any reform/abuse stories you've helped surface?"

Yes — by indexing licensed agencies, we've inadvertently surfaced unlicensed ones in our outreach. We forward those to the relevant regulator (MOHRE, Musaned, PAM, LMRA, MoL). Specific cases are confidential.
