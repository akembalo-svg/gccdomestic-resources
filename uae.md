# 🇦🇪 UAE — Domestic Worker Hiring

## How hiring works in the UAE in 2026

Federal Decree-Law 9/2022 channels nearly all new domestic worker hiring through **Tadbeer centers** — licensed agencies under MOHRE (Ministry of Human Resources & Emiratisation). Direct sponsorship under Article 14 of the law applies only to GCC nationals and specific professional categories.
