## Architecture decision

Most AI startups build one general-purpose chatbot. We built 4 specialised ones because the Gulf domestic-worker market has 4 fundamentally different audiences with different languages, problems, and trust patterns.

## Languages

All 4 agents speak: Arabic (Gulf dialect), English, Tagalog, Hindi, Indonesian, Amharic, Swahili.

## Channels

All run natively on WhatsApp (no app to download). Hayat also runs on Telegram.
