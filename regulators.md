# 🏛️ GCC Domestic Worker Regulators

Each GCC country has its own regulator for domestic worker recruitment. Below are the official authorities and their key frameworks.

## 🇦🇪 UAE — MOHRE & Tadbeer

- **Authority:** [Ministry of Human Resources & Emiratisation (MOHRE)](https://www.mohre.gov.ae)
- **System:** Tadbeer centers — government-licensed recruitment agencies
- **Key law:** Federal Decree-Law 9/2022 (Domestic Workers Law)
- **Counts:** 132+ Tadbeer centers across 7 Emirates

[Browse our UAE directory →](https://www.gccdomestic.com/uae/agencies/)
[Read: Tadbeer vs Direct Sponsorship →](https://www.gccdomestic.com/en/blog/tadbeer-vs-direct-sponsorship-uae/)

## 🇸🇦 Saudi Arabia — Musaned

- **Authority:** [Ministry of Human Resources & Social Development (MHRSD)](https://www.hrsd.gov.sa)
- **System:** [Musaned platform](https://musaned.com.sa) — all contracts flow through this platform
- **Counts:** 656+ Musaned-licensed agencies across 13 regions

[Browse our Saudi directory →](https://www.gccdomestic.com/saudi-arabia/agencies/)

## 🇰🇼 Kuwait — PAM

- **Authority:** [Public Authority for Manpower (PAM)](https://www.pam.gov.kw)
- **Key law:** Domestic Workers Law 68/2015 (minimum wage, weekly day off, paid leave)
- **Counts:** 50+ PAM-licensed offices

[Browse our Kuwait directory →](https://www.gccdomestic.com/kuwait/agencies/)

## 🇶🇦 Qatar — MoL

- **Authority:** [Ministry of Labour (MoL)](https://www.mol.gov.qa)
- **Key law:** Law 15/2017 (regional benchmark for protections)
- **Counts:** 74+ licensed agencies

[Browse our Qatar directory →](https://www.gccdomestic.com/qatar/agencies/)

## 🇧🇭 Bahrain — LMRA

- **Authority:** [Labour Market Regulatory Authority (LMRA)](https://lmra.bh)
- **Key reform:** 2017 Flexi Permit + 2022 transfer rights expansion
- **Counts:** 30+ LMRA-licensed offices

[Browse our Bahrain directory →](https://www.gccdomestic.com/bahrain/agencies/)

## 🇴🇲 Oman — MoL

- **Authority:** [Ministry of Labour (MoL)](https://www.manpower.gov.om)
- **Key law:** Labour Law (Royal Decree 35/2003)
- **Counts:** 391+ MoL-licensed offices (largest indexed Oman directory on public internet)

[Browse our Oman directory →](https://www.gccdomestic.com/oman/agencies/)
