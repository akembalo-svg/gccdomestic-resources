# 🇧🇭 Bahrain — Domestic Worker Hiring

## How hiring works in Bahrain in 2026

Bahrain's **LMRA** (Labour Market Regulatory Authority) licenses and supervises every domestic worker recruitment office. The 2017 Flexi Permit and 2022 reforms gave domestic workers stronger protections including labour-market transfer rights.
