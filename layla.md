# 🏢 Layla — Agency Onboarding Agent

Layla is the AI agent for **recruitment agencies** joining the GCC Domestic platform. If you run a Tadbeer center, Musaned-licensed agency, PAM office, or LMRA-licensed office and want to reach Gulf families directly — Layla is who you talk to first.

## What Layla does for agencies

- ✅ **License verification** — instant check against MOHRE/Musaned/PAM/LMRA/MoL portals
- 📸 **Worker profile creation** — guided video upload + QR-verified training certificate setup
- 👨‍👩‍👧 **Lead matching** — when a family asks Nadia for a specific worker type in your city, leads flow to you
- 📑 **Contract templates** — country-specific standard contracts auto-generated
- 💳 **Subscription management** — billing, plan changes, invoices

## Why agencies use Layla

- 🚫 **No app to install** — agency staff already use WhatsApp all day
- 💰 **Free leads forever** for the first 10 families per month — pay only when you convert beyond that
- 📈 **3x conversion vs direct calls** — families come pre-qualified by Nadia first
- ⏱️ **Onboarding in 24 hours** vs weeks of paperwork through traditional channels

## Quick onboarding flow

1. WhatsApp Layla — she asks for your license number + country
2. License verified against the government portal — usually 30 seconds
3. Add 3-5 worker profiles (name, nationality, photo, video, training certs)
4. Choose subscription tier
5. Your agency appears in the country directory within 1 hour

Email **info@gccdomestic.com** with:

- Agency name
