# 🇵🇭 Filipino Domestic Workers in the Gulf

The Philippines is the largest single source of skilled domestic workers in the Gulf. Filipino workers (locally called **OFWs** — Overseas Filipino Workers) are valued for English fluency, childcare certifications, and household management training.

## Why Gulf families choose Filipino workers

- 🗣️ **English-fluent** — significant advantage for expat families
- 👶 **Often certified in childcare** — many trained as TESDA-accredited
- 🍳 **International cuisine** — comfortable with both Western and Asian cooking
- 🤝 **Strong cultural fit** — Catholic majority, family-oriented, respectful

## How the Filipino hiring process works

1. **POEA verification** — every Filipino OFW must have a contract registered with the Philippine Overseas Employment Administration before departure
2. **Source agency in Philippines** pairs with the Gulf agency (Tadbeer/Musaned/PAM/LMRA/MoL)
3. **Worker arrives via OEC** (Overseas Employment Certificate)
4. **Embassy registration** within 7 days of arrival

Most Tadbeer centers and Musaned agencies have established Filipino corridors and can complete the process in **4-8 weeks** for UAE and **6-10 weeks** for Saudi.

## Common Filipino worker contract terms

- **Minimum wage:** set by Philippine embassy in each Gulf country
- **Day off:** weekly (Sunday traditional, but flexible)
- **Annual leave:** 30 days paid (UAE), 21 days (Qatar), per country law
- **Maximum work hours:** 10-12/day depending on country
- **Religious accommodation:** Filipino workers practice Catholicism — many request Sunday mass attendance
