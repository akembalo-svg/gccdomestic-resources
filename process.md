# 📋 The 7-Step Hiring Process — All 6 GCC Countries

This is the universal flow. Country-specific tweaks are noted where they matter.

## 1. Decide what you actually need

Live-in or live-out? Full-time or hourly? Housemaid, nanny, cook, driver, elderly care? Each has different regulatory paths and salary ranges.

**Tip:** if you need a nanny with childcare certifications, that narrows the agency pool significantly. Search filter on your country directory:
- [UAE](https://www.gccdomestic.com/uae/agencies/) · [Saudi](https://www.gccdomestic.com/saudi-arabia/agencies/) · [Kuwait](https://www.gccdomestic.com/kuwait/agencies/) · [Qatar](https://www.gccdomestic.com/qatar/agencies/) · [Bahrain](https://www.gccdomestic.com/bahrain/agencies/) · [Oman](https://www.gccdomestic.com/oman/agencies/)

## 2. Pick the right path

- **UAE:** Tadbeer (the only legal channel for new hires since 2022)
- **Saudi:** Musaned (every contract flows through here)
- **Kuwait:** PAM-licensed office
- **Qatar:** MoL-licensed agency
- **Bahrain:** LMRA-licensed office
- **Oman:** MoL-licensed office

Read more about each regulator: [Regulators page](/regulators/)

## 3. Filter agencies — three filters that matter

1. **Nationality availability:** Filipino, Indonesian, Ethiopian, Indian, Sri Lankan, Bangladeshi, Kenyan, Ugandan
2. **Location:** city matters for in-person interviews and faster paperwork
3. **Replacement policy:** 3 or 6-month window? Free or paid replacement?

## 4. WhatsApp 3-4 agencies (never phone)

Phone calls leave no paper trail. **Always WhatsApp.** You want everything in writing:
- Total package price (one-time + monthly salary)
- Replacement guarantee terms
- Worker video
- Government license number

Verify the license number against the [official regulator's portal](/regulators/) before paying anything.

## 5. Sign the contract

For UAE Tadbeer specifically: the contract is **standardized** by MOHRE. If the agency is asking you to sign something custom, that's a red flag.

Saudi Musaned: contracts auto-generate from the Musaned platform once both parties agree.

## 6. Wait for visa + medical + arrival

Typical timelines:
- UAE Tadbeer: **4-8 weeks** from contract to worker arrival
- Saudi Musaned: **6-12 weeks** (slower due to higher volume)
- Kuwait PAM: **4-6 weeks**
- Qatar/Oman/Bahrain: **6-10 weeks**

If anyone promises "overnight" or "tomorrow" delivery, that's an illegal back-channel. Do not pay.

## 7. Probation period — your safety net

Typically 3-6 months depending on country. During probation:
- Either party can end the contract
- The agency must offer a free replacement worker
- You can request a different nationality if the first didn't fit

This is when you actually validate the hire. Don't skip evaluating during probation.

## Costs to NEVER pay separately (red flags)

❌ "Visa fee" extra → already in the package
❌ "Express processing fee" → doesn't exist officially
❌ Cash without receipt
❌ Undisclosed "insurance top-up"
❌ "Translation fee" for the contract
❌ "Repatriation deposit" outside the Tadbeer/Musaned framework
