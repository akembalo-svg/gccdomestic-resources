# GCC Domestic Worker Hiring — Verification Cheat-Sheet 2026

If you hire domestic workers across more than one GCC country — family offices, multi-emirate households, expat families relocating — you need a single reference for verifying recruitment agency licences. **One page, six countries, five steps per country.**

## The 5-step pattern is the same everywhere

1. **Ask** the agency for its government licence number.
2. **Verify** it on the official regulator's register.
3. **Call** the regulator hotline to confirm current "Active" status.
4. **Cross-reference** on [gccdomestic.com](https://www.gccdomestic.com).
5. **Reject** cash-only or non-standard contract terms.

What changes country-by-country: the regulator name, register URL, hotline, and country-specific quirks.

## The single biggest cross-country tip

**Never pay in cash, never sign verbal-only agreements, never wire money to a personal bank account.** Every legitimate GCC recruitment agency uses official bank channels, prescribed contracts, and itemised receipts. The single most reliable signal of an illegitimate office is when they push for cash or off-contract terms.
