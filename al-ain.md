# 🌳 Hiring a Domestic Worker in Al Ain

Al Ain, the UAE's "Garden City" in the Abu Dhabi emirate's east, has ~10+ Tadbeer centers serving a primarily Emirati family demographic.

## Al Ain specifics

- 🇦🇪 **Emirati-majority demand** — preference for Muslim workers
- 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 **Large family households** — multi-worker demand
- 💰 **Competitive Abu Dhabi-area pricing**
