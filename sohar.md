# 🏭 Hiring a Domestic Worker in Sohar

Sohar, an industrial port city north of Muscat, has ~30+ MoL-licensed offices. Recruitment demand is steady due to the port and industrial zone (steel, petrochemicals) attracting expat workers and Omani professionals.
