# 💼 Nadia — Family-Facing Recruitment Agent

Nadia is the AI recruitment agent for families on the GCC Domestic platform. While [Hayat](/hayat/) is the household helper, **Nadia is the one who matches you with the right agency** when you start looking to hire.

## What Nadia does

- 🔍 **Agency matching** — recommends 3-5 verified Tadbeer/Musaned/PAM/LMRA/MoL agencies based on your needs
- 💰 **Salary benchmarking** — real-time salary ranges by nationality + country
- 📋 **License verification** — confirms every agency against the government's official portal before recommending
- 📅 **Process tracking** — visa, medical, Emirates ID, arrival — all status updates in one chat
- 🌐 **7 languages** — English, Arabic, Tagalog, Hindi, Indonesian, Amharic, Swahili

## How families use Nadia

1. *"I need a Filipino housemaid in Abu Dhabi, AED 2,000/month, available within 8 weeks"* → 5 matched Tadbeer centers with worker videos
2. *"What's the difference between Tadbeer and direct sponsorship?"* → clear explanation + cost comparison
3. *"This agency wants me to pay AED 5,000 cash without receipt. Red flag?"* → yes, here's the regulator's complaint form
4. *"Can you check if agency XYZ is really licensed?"* → instant verification against MOHRE Tadbeer portal

## Why Nadia exists (in addition to Hayat)

- **Hayat** = the household helper after you've hired
- **Nadia** = the recruitment agent helping you choose who to hire

Different stages, different specialization. Same WhatsApp number.
