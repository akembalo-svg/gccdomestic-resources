# What is Musaned?

**Musaned** (Arabic: مساند, "supportive") is the official electronic platform of Saudi Arabia's HRSD that regulates ~1,400 licensed domestic worker recruitment offices nationwide.

**Full definitional page:** https://www.gccdomestic.com/en/what-is-musaned/

## Quick facts

- 🇸🇦 Saudi Arabia
- Regulator: HRSD (Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development)
- Established: 2014 (mandatory since 2016)
- Platform: musaned.com.sa
- ~1,400 licensed offices across 13 regions
- Recruitment fee: SAR 12,000-22,000 (one-time)

## ⚠️ 2026 WPS e-salary mandate

From 1 January 2026, all domestic worker salaries in Saudi Arabia must be paid electronically via Musaned. Cash payment is no longer legal.

## Verify any office

1. musaned.com.sa → search by name or license number
2. Call HRSD on 19911 to confirm

## Vs Tadbeer (UAE)

Musaned is a centralised platform. [Tadbeer](https://www.gccdomestic.com/en/what-is-tadbeer/) is a network of physical centres. Same purpose, different structures.
