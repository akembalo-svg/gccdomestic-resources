# 🏛️ Hiring a Domestic Worker in Abu Dhabi

Abu Dhabi has 30+ Tadbeer centers and represents about 25% of the UAE's domestic worker recruitment market. The audience here skews more Emirati than Dubai, with different cultural preferences and slightly different pricing patterns.

## Why Abu Dhabi is different

- 🇦🇪 **Higher Emirati family ratio** — preference for Muslim workers (Indonesian, Bangladeshi, Ethiopian Muslim)
- 🏫 **Childcare focus** — high demand for certified nannies due to professional dual-income households
- 💵 **Stable salary range** — less variance than Dubai but slightly lower top-end
- 🏛️ **Government employee discounts** — some Tadbeer centers offer reduced fees for federal employees

## Cost in Abu Dhabi (2026)

| Service | One-time fee | Monthly salary |
|---|---|---|
| Filipino housemaid | AED 8,000–15,000 | AED 1,500–2,400 |
| Indonesian housemaid | AED 7,000–13,000 | AED 1,400–2,200 |
| Ethiopian housemaid (Muslim) | AED 6,000–11,000 | AED 1,000–1,500 |
| Filipino nanny (certified) | AED 10,000–18,000 | AED 1,800–2,800 |

## Top areas with Tadbeer clusters

- **Khalidiyah / Tourist Club:** largest concentration
- **Mussafah:** affordable mid-tier centers
- **Al Reem Island / Saadiyat:** premium centers for high-end residences
- **Al Ain:** dedicated regional centers (10+)
