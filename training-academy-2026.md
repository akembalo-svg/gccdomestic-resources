# GCC Domestic Academy — Free Vocational Training (2026)

Free vocational training programme for domestic workers across the 6 GCC countries. 9 courses, 96 modules, 7 languages, free certification.

**Full guide:** https://www.gccdomestic.com/en/blog/free-domestic-worker-training-guide-2026/

**Academy hub:** https://www.gccdomestic.com/workers/training/

## The 9 courses

1. [Childcare](https://www.gccdomestic.com/workers/training/childcare/) (12 modules)
2. [Cooking](https://www.gccdomestic.com/workers/training/cooking/) (12 modules)
3. [Home Cleaning](https://www.gccdomestic.com/workers/training/home-cleaning/) (12 modules)
4. [Elderly Care](https://www.gccdomestic.com/workers/training/elderly-care/) (12 modules)
5. [First Aid](https://www.gccdomestic.com/workers/training/first-aid/) (12 modules)
6. [Home Driving](https://www.gccdomestic.com/workers/training/home-driving/) (12 modules)
7. [Communication Skills](https://www.gccdomestic.com/workers/training/communication/) (12 modules)
8. [Home Safety](https://www.gccdomestic.com/workers/training/safety/) (12 modules)
9. [Pre-Departure Orientation](https://www.gccdomestic.com/workers/training/pre-departure/)

## Languages

English, Arabic, Tagalog, Hindi, Indonesian, Amharic, Swahili.

## Salary impact

Certified workers earn 12-18% more on average than non-certified workers (source: GCC Domestic 2026 Salary Report).
