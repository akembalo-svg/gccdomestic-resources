# 🌊 Hiring a Domestic Worker in Muscat

Muscat hosts the largest concentration of Oman's 391+ MoL-licensed recruitment offices — about 150+ operate within the capital. Oman has one of the more standardized recruitment markets in the GCC, with Royal Decree 35/2003 providing the framework.

## Muscat specifics

- 🌍 **Multi-national household demand** — Omani families + significant Indian/Pakistani/Bangladeshi expat community
- 💰 **Most affordable in the GCC** — lowest one-time fees and monthly salaries
- 🤝 **Smooth recruitment process** — Ministry of Labour digital portal is efficient
- 🏝️ **Tourism + diplomatic expat mix**
