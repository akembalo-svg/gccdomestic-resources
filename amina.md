# 🌐 Amina — 24/7 Support Agent

Amina is the **always-on support agent** for everyone on the GCC Domestic platform — families, agencies, and workers. While [Hayat](/hayat/), [Nadia](/nadia/), and [Layla](/layla/) handle specialized workflows, Amina is the one you ping when something goes wrong at 2 AM.

## What Amina does

- 🚨 **Real-time agency status checks** — is this agency still licensed? Last verified?
- 💸 **Refund / replacement guidance** — country-specific procedures for the 4 main scenarios
- ⚖️ **Salary dispute escalation** — how to file a complaint with MOHRE/PAM/LMRA/MoL
- 📞 **Worker rights advisory** — country-specific rights and complaint hotlines
- 🌍 **8 languages** — Arabic, English, Tagalog, Hindi, Amharic, Swahili, Indonesian, Urdu

## Common Amina requests

1. *"My maid arrived but she's not the same person from the video. What now?"* → identity-fraud escalation steps
2. *"Agency hasn't responded for 5 days. Salary delayed."* → Tadbeer/Musaned dispute filing
3. *"My worker wants to go home early. Replacement process?"* → 3-6 month replacement window guidance
4. *"My visa renewal is due. What documents?"* → country-specific checklist

## Why 24/7 matters

Most disputes happen outside business hours — late evenings, weekends, public holidays. Traditional agencies close at 5 PM. Amina doesn't. The most common Amina conversations happen 10 PM – 2 AM.
