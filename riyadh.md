# 🏛️ Hiring a Domestic Worker in Riyadh

Riyadh is Saudi Arabia's capital and the country's largest domestic worker recruitment market — 200+ of Saudi's 656+ Musaned-licensed agencies operate in the city. Every contract flows through Musaned (musaned.com.sa).

## Riyadh specifics

- 🕌 **Conservative environment** — strong preference for Muslim workers
- 👨‍👧 **Large household demand** — Saudi families often hire multiple workers
- 🚗 **Driver demand high** — women in Riyadh increasingly drive (post-2018 reform) but family drivers still very common
- 💵 **Pricing variance is large** — agencies range from budget to premium
