# 🇱🇰 Sri Lankan Domestic Workers in the Gulf

Sri Lanka has a 40+ year history of sending domestic workers to the Gulf. Today most Sri Lankan workers come from rural Sinhalese or Tamil-speaking regions, with strong specialization in housemaid and elderly care roles.

## Why Gulf families choose Sri Lankan workers

- 🗣️ **English/Tamil-speaking** — useful for Tamil-speaking Gulf households and English-speaking expats
- 👵 **Elderly care expertise** — Sri Lankan workers are highly valued for senior care
- 🍳 **South Asian cuisine** — comfortable with rice-based diets common across the Gulf
- ✝️☪️🕉️ **Religious diversity** — Buddhist majority but Hindu, Muslim, and Christian workers all available

## Sri Lankan hiring process

1. **SLBFE (Sri Lanka Bureau of Foreign Employment)** registration required for every departing worker
2. **Source agency in Sri Lanka** must be SLBFE-licensed
3. **Pre-departure training** at SLBFE-certified centers (mandatory since 2013)
4. **Worker arrives via Bandaranaike International Airport**
5. **Embassy registration** in destination country

Timeline: **5-9 weeks** typically.

## Post-2013 reform context

After multiple high-profile abuse cases in the early 2010s, Sri Lanka instituted stronger protections:
- Mandatory pre-departure training (2 weeks)
- Standard contract templates required
- Minimum age raised to 25 for KSA, 23 for other GCC
- Family approval required if worker has children under 5 (the "F-Form" rule)
