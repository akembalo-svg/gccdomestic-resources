# 🇸🇦 Saudi Arabia — Domestic Worker Hiring

## How hiring works in Saudi Arabia in 2026

Saudi Arabia channels all foreign domestic worker recruitment through **Musaned** (musaned.com.sa) — the official platform of the Ministry of Human Resources & Social Development. Every contract, payment, and worker transfer happens within the Musaned ecosystem.
