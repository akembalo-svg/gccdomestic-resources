# 🏔️ Hiring a Domestic Worker in Ras Al Khaimah (RAK)

Ras Al Khaimah, the UAE's northernmost emirate, has ~15+ Tadbeer centers serving residents of RAK plus families in Umm Al Quwain and Fujairah who travel for recruitment.

## RAK specifics

- 💰 **Affordable pricing** — comparable to Ajman
- 🌐 **Tourism + emerging family destination** — recruitment market growing
- 🤝 **Established Filipino + Indonesian corridors**

## Cost in RAK (2026)

| Service | One-time fee | Monthly salary |
|---|---|---|
| Filipino housemaid | AED 8,500–14,000 | AED 1,500–2,400 |
| Indonesian housemaid | AED 7,500–12,000 | AED 1,300–2,000 |
| Ethiopian housemaid | AED 5,500–10,000 | AED 1,000–1,500 |
