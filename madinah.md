# 🕌 Hiring a Domestic Worker in Madinah (Medina)

Madinah, Saudi Arabia's second holiest city, has ~40+ Musaned-licensed agencies. Like Makkah, the recruitment market is shaped by religious tourism and a residents' strong preference for Muslim workers.

## Madinah specifics

- ☪️ **Muslim worker preference** dominates
- 🕌 **Steady year-round demand** with Ramadan peaks
- 🏨 **Hospitality + private household mix**
- 💰 **Affordable mid-tier pricing**
