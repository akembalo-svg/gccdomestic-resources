# 🇸🇦 Musaned — Saudi Arabia's Domestic Worker Platform

**Musaned** (مساند) is the official platform of Saudi Arabia's Ministry of Human Resources & Social Development. Every contract for foreign domestic workers must flow through it. There is no legal alternative.

## Why Musaned exists

Launched in 2017, Musaned consolidated what was previously fragmented across:
- Hundreds of independent recruitment offices
- Multiple ministry portals
- Bilateral country corridors that didn't sync

It now standardizes:
- Worker selection (with source-country pre-approval)
- Contract templates
- Payment processing
- Dispute resolution
- Visa + iqama (residence permit) integration

## Process via Musaned

1. **Family registers** on musaned.com.sa (via Absher account)
2. **Choose agency** from licensed list
3. **Worker selection** with profile + video
4. **Contract auto-generated** — both parties e-sign in Musaned
5. **Pay deposit** via SADAD
6. **Visa issued** by Saudi Embassy in source country
7. **Worker arrival** + iqama issuance
8. **Monthly salary** auto-tracked in Musaned

## Verifying an agency

1. Login to musaned.com.sa
2. Search by agency name or location
3. Cross-reference with our directory: [656+ verified agencies](https://www.gccdomestic.com/saudi-arabia/agencies/)

If an "agency" in Saudi Arabia is NOT in Musaned, the contract is not legally enforceable and the worker would be in violation of immigration law.
