# 🇪🇹 Ethiopian Domestic Workers in the Gulf

Ethiopia is one of the fastest-growing source countries for Gulf domestic workers. Following the **2018 lifting of the migration ban** and the new bilateral agreements with KSA, UAE, and Kuwait, Ethiopian workers are now a major part of Gulf households.

## Why Gulf families choose Ethiopian workers

- 💰 **Competitive salaries** — typically 30-40% lower than Filipino equivalents
- 🙏 **Strong work ethic** — long-standing reputation across the Gulf
- 🍳 **Adaptable to Gulf cuisine** — comfortable with Arabic and African foods
- ☪️ **Religious diversity** — both Christian (Ethiopian Orthodox) and Muslim workers available — many Saudi families prefer Muslim Ethiopians

## Ethiopian hiring process

1. **MoL (Ethiopia Ministry of Labour & Social Affairs) clearance** — every worker must be processed through licensed Ethiopian recruitment agencies
2. **Pre-departure training** — Ethiopian workers complete 5-day orientation including basic Arabic
3. **Source agency pairs with Gulf agency** through bilateral corridor
4. **Worker arrives via Bole International Airport** (Addis Ababa)
5. **Welfare attaché registration** at the Ethiopian embassy in the destination country

Typical timeline: **6-10 weeks** for UAE/Saudi/Kuwait.

## Post-2018 reform context

After Ethiopia lifted its 2013-2018 migration ban, both Ethiopia and Gulf countries strengthened bilateral protections:

- **Bilateral agreements** with KSA (2017), Lebanon (2020), and Jordan (2018)
- **Standard employment contracts** required
- **Pre-departure orientation** mandatory (5 days)
- **Welfare attachés** at embassies in each Gulf country
- **Banned:** passport confiscation, salary deduction for "transport costs"
