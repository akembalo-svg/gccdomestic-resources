# 🇧🇭 Bahrain LMRA — Labour Market Regulatory Authority

LMRA (Labour Market Regulatory Authority) was established in 2006 and is now Bahrain's primary regulator for all foreign labour. Bahrain pioneered regional reforms with the **2017 Flexi Permit** (allowing workers to work without a sponsor) and **2022 transfer reforms** that strengthened domestic worker mobility.

## What LMRA regulates

- All recruitment office licensing
- Worker visa issuance via Bahrain Visa portal
- Standard employment contracts
- Salary dispute resolution
- Flexi Permit administration (for eligible categories)

## Key reforms

**2017 Flexi Permit:**
- Allows workers to work for multiple employers
- Workers pay LMRA directly for their own permit
- Reduced dependency on single sponsor

**2022 Transfer Reforms:**
- Workers can transfer between sponsors with conditions
- Stronger anti-trafficking provisions
- Mandatory mediation before contract termination

## Process via LMRA

1. Family contacts an LMRA-licensed office
2. Office secures visa via Bahrain Visa portal
3. Worker arrives + medical
4. CPR (Central Population Register) ID issued
5. Standard LMRA contract signed
6. Monthly salary paid (bank transfer recommended for record)
