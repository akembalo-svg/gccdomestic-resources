# 🇶🇦🇴🇲 Qatar + Oman — Ministry of Labour Systems

Qatar and Oman both regulate domestic workers through their respective **Ministry of Labour** (MoL / MoLSW). Each has its own legal framework but they share many procedural similarities — covered together here.

## 🇶🇦 Qatar — Law 15/2017

This law set the **regional benchmark** for domestic worker protections in 2017. Key provisions:

- ✅ Maximum 10 hours work/day
- ✅ Mandatory weekly day off
- ✅ 3 weeks paid annual leave
- ✅ End-of-service payment (3 weeks per year)
- ✅ Workers can change employers after contract expiry without sponsor permission (2020 reform)
- ✅ Banned: passport confiscation
- ✅ Workers can file complaints with ADLSA (Administrative Development, Labour & Social Affairs)

**Complaint hotline:** 16008

## 🇴🇲 Oman — Royal Decree 35/2003 + supplementary regulations

Oman's foundational labour law was issued in 2003 with multiple updates since. For domestic workers specifically:

- ✅ Standard contract template required (from MoL)
- ✅ Weekly day off (1 day)
- ✅ Annual leave (30 days after 1 year)
- ✅ End-of-service indemnity
- ✅ Worker keeps passport
- ✅ MoL-administered dispute resolution

**Complaint hotline:** 80077000

## Process (same for both)

1. Family contacts a MoL-licensed office in their country
2. Office secures visa
3. Worker arrives + medical
4. Civil ID / Resident Card issued
5. Standard MoL contract signed
6. Monthly salary paid

## Verifying an office

- **Qatar:** check ADLSA portal or call 16008
- **Oman:** check manpower.gov.om or call 80077000
