# 🇰🇼 Kuwait — Domestic Worker Hiring

## How hiring works in Kuwait in 2026

Kuwait regulates domestic worker recruitment through **PAM** (Public Authority for Manpower). Foreign recruitment must go through PAM-licensed offices. The 2015 Domestic Workers Law guarantees minimum wage, paid leave, and a weekly day off.
